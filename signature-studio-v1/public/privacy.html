Signature Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Signature Studio is a Modern Gentlemen service for creating, saving, and installing email signatures.

Information you provide

Projects may contain your name, role, company, contact details, social links, logo, icons, colors, and layout choices.

Cloud saves

When you use cloud saving, project data is stored with Supabase. Row-level access controls are configured so signed-in users can access only their own project records. Images intended for email signatures may be publicly accessible so recipients' email clients can retrieve them.

Gmail

The default Gmail installation workflow does not request access to your Google account. It copies the signature and opens Gmail Settings for you to paste it. Optional direct Gmail installation, if enabled in the future, may request the Gmail settings permission needed to update a signature; it does not require reading message bodies or sending email.
