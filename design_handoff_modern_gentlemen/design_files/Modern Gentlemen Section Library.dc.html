THE LONG READ

The Monochrome Wardrobe, Engineered

BY A. BELLAMY·SUMMER 2026·9 MIN READ

Agentleman's wardrobe should read like a well-kept garage — everything in its place, nothing for show. When you strip colour out of the equation, what remains is proportion, cloth, and the quiet confidence of a man who has already decided who he is. This is not minimalism for its own sake; it is a system, engineered for mornings that ask nothing of you. Black, charcoal, bone, and the occasional deep racing green — a palette that travels, layers, and never argues with itself across a long week.

11 The Debate OPINION · TWO OPPOSING COLUMNS
THE DEBATE

Should a watch ever match the belt?

For

Cohesion is the whole point.

Leather answers to leather. A brown strap over a black belt is the sartorial equivalent of a missed gear-change — small, but everyone in the room felt it.

— J. HART, STYLE EDITOR
Against

Rules are for the insecure.

A watch is inherited, chosen, lived-in. Asking it to obey your belt is asking the most personal thing you own to become an accessory. Wear the watch. Forget the belt.

— A. BELLAMY, EDITOR-AT-LARGE

Henri Vasseur
MASTER WATCHMAKER
You could machine these parts in a day. Why spend three weeks by hand?

Because the hand knows something the machine doesn't — when to stop. A movement finished by hand carries a decision on every surface. That's what you're buying.

Is there room for that patience in 2026?

More than ever. The faster the world moves, the more people want one object that refuses to.
